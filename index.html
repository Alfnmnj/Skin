A 12-month, low-clutter plan built around the visible concerns you described: sun-related uneven tone, combination facial skin, dryness/tightness and dry body/feet.

6 core products · add slowly · no scrub
Goal
Move toward your natural baseline tone, improve texture and keep the skin barrier comfortable. This is not a skin-whitening plan.

The 12-month map

Buy only when needed. Prices are snapshots and can change.
01
Repair

Stop regular soap on face/dry areas. Cleanse gently. Moisturize after washing. SPF 50+ every morning.

02
Protect

Keep the same routine. Focus on enough sunscreen and outdoor protection. Do not add multiple actives.

03
Introduce

If tightness/flaking is controlled, start azelaic acid 10% 2–3 nights/week.

04
Build

Increase azelaic acid gradually if comfortable. Keep moisturizer and sunscreen consistent.

05
Steady

No new products. Photograph the forehead monthly in the same lighting to judge change.

06
Review

Expect gradual change, not instant lightening. If pigmentation is strong or worsening, consider a dermatologist.

07
Optimize

Continue the core routine. Only consider a retinoid with professional guidance if your barrier is stable.

08
Maintain

Keep the routine boring. Consistency beats adding another serum.

09
Protect

Check sunscreen stock. Replace expired/old products. Keep hats/umbrella in your outdoor kit.

10
Refine

If comfortable, continue the same active schedule. Do not stack acids, scrubs and retinoids.

11
Measure

Compare month-1 and month-11 photos. Judge tone, texture, dryness and irritation—not just colour.

12
Maintain

Keep the smallest routine that works: gentle cleanser, moisturizer, SPF, azelaic acid if tolerated.

Daily operating system

Keep this page open while you build the habit.
AM / FACE

01 Clean

Gentle cleanser or water.

02 Moisturize

Ceramide moisturizer, especially on tight areas.

03 Protect

SPF 50+ every day. Hat/umbrella/shade outdoors.

PM / FACE

01 Clean

Gentle cleanser.

02 Treat

From month 3: azelaic acid 10% 2–3 nights/week, then increase only if comfortable.

03 Repair

Moisturizer. Petroleum jelly only on very dry patches.

BODY / FEET

01 Shower

Replace harsh regular soap on dry areas with a gentle wash. Avoid very hot water.

02 Seal

Body cream immediately after shower.

03 Feet

10% urea nightly on dry/thick heels; petroleum jelly on top if needed.

The rule: a product that repeatedly burns, stings or leaves you tight is not “working harder.” Reduce frequency or stop it. Irritation can make uneven pigmentation harder to improve.

Hard rules

NO DIY bleaching.

No lemon, baking soda, harsh scrubs or unregulated “fairness/whitening” creams.

Don't stack actives.

For this skin pattern, more products can mean more irritation and more pigmentation. Introduce one change at a time.

Sun protection is treatment.

SPF only works if applied adequately and reapplied during prolonged outdoor exposure. Clothing and shade help too.

When to see a dermatologist

Book an evaluation if the forehead pigmentation is rapidly changing, sharply patterned, itchy/scaly, painful, bleeding/crusting, or not improving despite 3–6 months of good sun protection and a gentle routine. A dermatologist can distinguish tanning from melasma, post-inflammatory pigmentation or another condition and can prescribe stronger treatments when appropriate.

This page is an educational shopping/routine plan, not a diagnosis or prescription. Patch-test new products and follow the product label.
